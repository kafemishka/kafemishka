Arrange

A private helper page (not linked anywhere on the site) for setting the order of "finished pieces" on the Ceramics page.

  1. Drag the photos into the order you want. Number 1 shows first.
  2. Click Copy order.
  3. On GitHub, open ceramics-order.txt, click the pencil, select everything (Cmd+A), paste (Cmd+V), and click Commit changes.
